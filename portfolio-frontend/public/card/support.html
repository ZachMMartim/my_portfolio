SUPPORT

Aleator · an idle poker deckbuilder

Found a bug or stuck on something? Email brendanbeckstrom1@gmail.com. Include the VERSION line from Settings → ABOUT, and a screenshot if you can.

WHERE IS MY PROGRESS SAVED?

On your device, and on iPhone a copy is kept in your iCloud account. Signed in to iCloud, a reinstall or a new iPhone picks up where you left off. In the browser, progress lives in that browser only.

DO MY BOTS PLAY WHILE THE APP IS CLOSED?

Yes, up to your offline cap, which is shown on the BOTS tab. When you come back, WELCOME BACK shows what they earned.

WHY IS THERE NO SOUND?

The game starts silent. Open Settings (≡, top right) and raise MASTER VOLUME.

HOW DO I START OVER?

Settings → DATA → RESET PROGRESS. This cannot be undone.

ARE THERE PURCHASES OR ADS?

There are no ads. In the iPhone app, gems can be earned by playing or bought as optional in-app purchases; nothing in the game needs a purchase. The browser version sells nothing.

HOW DO I GET A REFUND FOR A PURCHASE?

Purchases are made and refunded by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
