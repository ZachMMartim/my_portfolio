PRIVACY POLICY

Aleator · Effective October 10, 2026

Aleator does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Your game progress and your settings are saved on your device. On iPhone, a copy of your progress is also kept in your own iCloud account, so it survives reinstalling the game or moving to a new phone. That copy is stored by Apple under your Apple ID; we never receive it and cannot see it. In the browser version, progress is saved in that browser's local storage instead.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing. The app sends no data to us or to anyone else — the iCloud copy above goes only to your own account, and a daily score goes only to Game Center when you choose to post it (below) — and it contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple handles that under Apple's privacy policy.

THE DAILY BOARD

The daily puzzle's leaderboard runs on Apple's Game Center. Nothing is posted unless you choose to: after a daily run the game asks, and Settings → POST DAILY SCORES keeps your answer. A posted score appears on a public leaderboard with your Game Center nickname, where any Game Center player of Aleator can see it. Apple stores the leaderboard under Apple's privacy policy; the game only reads it back to show you the board, and never sees your Apple ID. A score already posted stays on that day's board, and the boards reset every day. You can play the daily without Game Center, unranked.

YOUR AGE RANGE

Some US states require apps to check a player's age category through the App Store. If Apple tells the game that such a law applies to your Apple Account, the game asks Apple for your age range (under 13, 13–15, 16–17 or 18 and over) using Apple's Declared Age Range feature, and Apple may ask you, or a parent, to share it. The game uses the answer only to meet those laws and forgets it straight away. The one thing it keeps, on your device, is the date it last asked, so that it asks at most once a year. Nothing is sent anywhere. Apple handles your age and any parental consent under Apple's privacy policy.

THE BROWSER VERSION

The browser version is served from a website. Like any website, its hosting provider may process standard request information, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the page. The game itself sends nothing.

CHILDREN

Aleator collects no information from anyone, including children. Where the law requires the game to ask for a child's age range, the answer is handled on the device as described above.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change with it.
